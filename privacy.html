Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026.

Hermes Workspace is a private, self-hosted integration operated by Endian Tribe for its owner.

Google data accessed

With the account owner's consent, Hermes Workspace may access Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, and Google Tasks data needed to carry out the owner's requests.

How data is used

Google user data is used only to provide the requested Workspace functionality in the self-hosted Hermes environment. It is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties for their own purposes.

Credentials and storage

OAuth credentials are stored on the owner's self-hosted system in a protected local credential store. Application data may be retained only as needed for the owner's workflows and system operation.

Revocation and deletion

The account owner may revoke Google OAuth access at any time through their Google Account security settings and may remove locally stored credentials from the self-hosted system.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to admin@endiantribe.com.
