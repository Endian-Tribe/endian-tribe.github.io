Hermes Workspace

Hermes Workspace is a private, self-hosted Google Workspace integration operated by Endian Tribe for its owner. It is not offered as a public consumer service.

The integration uses Google OAuth to access Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Tasks only after the account owner grants permission. Access is used to perform actions the owner explicitly requests through the self-hosted Hermes system.
